## 📌 Course Overview & Structure

* **Course Code:** TH5 (B)
* **Course Title:** Leadership and Management Skills
* **Total Periods:** 60 (4 Periods/Week)
* **Objective:** To develop essential leadership traits, effective communication, time management, and problem-solving skills necessary for professional environments.
