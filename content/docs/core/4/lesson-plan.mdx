## 📌 Course Overview & Structure

* **Course Code:** TH4 (B)
* **Course Title:** IoT Communication Technology
* **Category:** Professional Elective
* **Total Periods:** 60 (4 Periods/Week over 15 Weeks)
* **Objective:** To equip students with a deep understanding of the communication protocols, network architectures, and wireless technologies (from short-range to LPWAN) that facilitate data exchange in IoT ecosystems.
